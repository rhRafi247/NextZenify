FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

Answers to common questions regarding our development process, tech stack, and engagement policies.

How do you manage project communication and sprints? +

We work in 2-week Agile sprints with regular async updates via Slack/Discord and bi-weekly live video demos. You receive direct access to GitHub/GitLab repositories and Jira/Linear boards.

Do I own the intellectual property and source code? +

Yes, 100%. Upon milestone completion and settlement, all source code, IP rights, designs, database schemas, and digital assets belong entirely to your company.

Can NextZenify work with our existing codebase and in-house team? +

Absolutely. We frequently augment existing teams, conduct codebase refactoring, migrate legacy systems to modern architectures, or build brand-new standalone features.

What is your typical turnaround time for an MVP? +

A production-ready MVP typically takes between 4 to 8 weeks depending on complexity. Enterprise platforms and large-scale SaaS systems generally follow a 3 to 6-month roadmap.
